Apply db/migrations/003_editorial_history.sql in one transaction on a staging branch, verify that existing slugs resolve, then on primary. A short write lock serializes the initial alias backfill with existing publishers. The trigger reserves each historic slug for its article and archives the previous article payload on updates from every client. Authoritative article rows stay in blog_articles. A deleted article and its dependent history are removed together. For application rollback, redeploy the previous build and leave the additive tables and history intact. To disable recording, remove the blog_article_history trigger in a maintenance transaction after backing up both new tables. Do not drop editorial records during rollback.
